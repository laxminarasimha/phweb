A hospital rooted in personal care.

Prasanthi Hospitals was founded by Dr. C.N. Murthy, BAMS, in Governorpet, Vijayawada. He continues to provide family physician consultations at the hospital, working alongside Dr. C.S.K. Aditya, M.D. (General Medicine).

Modern medical care is at the centre of the hospital's work. Our integrative approach also includes Ayurvedic consultations with Dr. Nishteshwar, M.D. (Ayurveda), while keeping personal attention to each patient's needs at the heart of care.

✚

Care in a familiar setting.

Our facilities support outpatient consultations, daycare, acute medical inpatient care and selected surgical work. A laboratory and pharmacy support the hospital's day-to-day care.

When a patient needs ICU care or treatment beyond our facilities, referral to a hospital with the appropriate services is arranged.

Modern medical care with personal attention.

Our services support outpatient care, observation, medical admissions and selected procedures, with referral when care beyond our facilities is required.

01

Outpatient consultations

General medicine and family physician consultations with Dr. C.S.K. Aditya and Dr. C.N. Murthy.

Ayurvedic consultations with Dr. Nishteshwar are also available. Call to confirm his consultation time.

02

Daycare

Care, treatment and observation with admission and discharge on the same day when recommended by the treating doctor.

The doctor will advise whether daycare or inpatient admission is appropriate.

03

Medical inpatient care

Acute medical admissions within the hospital's available facilities.

Patients requiring ICU care or treatment beyond these facilities are referred to an appropriate hospital.

04

Surgical care

Selected surgical procedures within the hospital's facilities, following consultation with the attending surgeon.

Contact the hospital about surgeon availability and the procedure you wish to discuss.

05

Laboratory services

Routine laboratory tests are performed in-house. Specialised investigations are arranged through external laboratories when needed.

Call ahead for test availability and preparation instructions.

06

On-site pharmacy

The hospital has a pharmacy supporting prescribed treatment for its patients.

07

Insurance enquiries

Speak with the hospital team about documentation for an insurance claim and your insurer's requirements.

Please confirm your policy's coverage and any cashless arrangements before admission.

08

Patient support

Our hospital team can help with appointment enquiries, doctor availability and information needed before your visit.

Plan your visit.

Call or WhatsApp the hospital team to confirm doctor availability, consultation timings and appointment details before travelling.

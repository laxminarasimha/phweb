Hospital services

Modern medical care with personal attention, supported by an integrative approach that includes Ayurvedic consultations.

Outpatient consultations

General medicine and family physician consultations with Dr. C.S.K. Aditya and Dr. C.N. Murthy.

Ayurvedic consultations with Dr. Nishteshwar are available as part of the hospital's integrative approach. Call to confirm his consultation time.

Doctor information →

Daycare

Care, treatment and observation with admission and discharge on the same day when recommended by the treating doctor.

The doctor will advise whether daycare or an inpatient admission is appropriate.

Medical inpatient care

Acute medical admissions within the hospital's available facilities.

Patients who need ICU care or treatment beyond these facilities are referred to a hospital with the appropriate services.

Surgical care

Selected surgical procedures within the hospital's facilities, following consultation with the attending surgeon.

Contact the hospital about the surgeon's availability and the procedure you wish to discuss.

Laboratory services

Routine laboratory tests are performed in-house. Specialised investigations are arranged through external laboratories when needed.

Call ahead for test availability and any preparation instructions.

On-site pharmacy

The hospital has a pharmacy supporting prescribed treatment for its patients.

Insurance enquiries

Speak with the hospital team about documentation for an insurance claim and your insurer's requirements.

Please confirm your policy's coverage and any cashless arrangements before admission.
